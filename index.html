The journey

One country, six chapters

This is a beautiful, ambitious route. Travel days are designed around high-speed trains, with private cars only where they save time and spare you the luggage scramble, especially Naples to Sorrento and the airport legs.

Bay of Naples · lemon groves · cliff towns

The romantic pause

Sorrento is the base: walkable, beautiful, and the right launch point for Positano, Amalfi, and Ravello. We recommend a private car for both Naples transfers. The Circumvesuviana is cheap, but it is crowded and awkward with suitcases.

  • 26 Sep 08:50 Frecciarossa Rome → Naples (~1h10). Private driver to the hotel by late morning.
  • Afternoon Piazza Tasso, old town, Marina Grande viewpoints, first lemon-scented evening.
  • 27 Sep Full-day Amalfi Coast: Positano, Amalfi, Ravello, and coastal belvederes.
  • Evening Return to Sorrento. Romantic dinner with a view. This is the night to linger.
  • Optional Pompeii or a private boat. With only two nights, the coast should come first; Pompeii is the piece to drop if you want a slower day.
Piazza Tasso Positano Amalfi Ravello Pompeii Boat along the coast

The Renaissance, then her birthday in Chianti

City of light, and the longest stay

Three nights give Florence room to breathe. Day one is the historic centre. Day two is art and sunset. Day three, 30 September, is entirely hers: a private convertible through Chianti, then a reserved birthday table in the evening.

  • 28 Sep Private Sorrento → Naples, then Frecciarossa to Firenze S.M.N. (~3h). Afternoon: Duomo, Piazza della Signoria, Ponte Vecchio.
  • 29 Sep 09:00 Uffizi Gallery (timed tickets). Afternoon free in the centre. Sunset at Piazzale Michelangelo.
  • 30 Sep Full-day private convertible: Greve, Castellina, San Gimignano, Siena, photo stops, Tuscan lunch. Return for dinner.

Alternatives

Segno Rooftop Experience: Duomo and terracotta-roof sunset, vegetable “Radici” tasting. About €90–120 / C$140–187 pp plus wine. More panoramic, slightly less formal. ~€380–450 / C$593–702 for two.

Antesi at Villa San Michele, Fiesole: eight tables in a 16th-century loggia above Florence. Vegetarian “Traccia” menu €160 / C$250 pp, wine pairing €110 / C$172. Taxi each way ~€40 / C$62. The most secluded historic setting. ~€720 / C$1,123 including transfers.

Cliffs, colour, and one unforgettable sunset

The Ligurian night

Morning train Florence → La Spezia → Manarola. The village is steep; we only recommend stays that are close to the station or that offer luggage transfer. One night is enough for Manarola, Vernazza, and sunset, not for hiking all five villages.

  • 08:00 Train Firenze S.M.N. → La Spezia Centrale (~2h20), then 8 minutes to Manarola.
  • Late morning Porter or short, manageable walk to the room. Leave large bags if needed.
  • Afternoon Manarola harbour and viewpoints, then the Cinque Terre train to Vernazza.
  • Optional Via dell’Amore, Riomaggiore → Manarola. Open in 2026 with timed tickets; book ahead. Flat, about 30 minutes.
  • Sunset Back in Manarola for the classic harbour light, then dinner on a terrace.

Arrive afternoon · the city after dark

Grand Canal to San Marco

This is the longest overland day. An early Cinque Terre departure reaches Venezia Santa Lucia around early afternoon. Staying beside the station means you never drag bags over bridges. Then the vaporetto does the theatre for you: a slow Grand Canal ride to St Mark’s.

  • 07:00 Manarola → La Spezia → Venezia S. Lucia, about 4.5–5.5 hours with one change.
  • 14:00 Hotel drop. Vaporetto from Ferrovia down the Grand Canal.
  • Afternoon Rialto, St Mark’s Square, St Mark’s Basilica, Doge’s Palace (pre-booked).
  • Evening Quiet back-canal walk. Optional private gondola after 7:00 PM (€110 / C$172 for the boat).

The route

How you move

Transportation

Private where it matters

Fiumicino → Rome hotel (€80 / C$125). Naples Centrale ↔ Sorrento hotel both ways (€150 / C$234 each). These three legs are the difference between a graceful trip and a suitcase ordeal.

High-speed trains for the rest

Rome–Naples 1h10 · Naples–Florence ~3h · Florence–Manarola ~2h40 · Manarola–Venice 4.5–5.5h · Venice–Milan 2h20. Book Frecciarossa 60–90 days ahead.

Cinque Terre & Venice water

Cinque Terre Train Card for village hops. In Venice, a 24-hour vaporetto pass from Ferrovia. Private water taxi from Santa Lucia is optional (~€80–120 / C$125–187) if you prefer a cinematic arrival.

4 October · Malpensa

Malpensa Express Centrale → MXP T1, €13 / C$20 pp, ~50 minutes. Leave the hotel at 09:00 for a 1:00 PM Toronto flight. Private car upgrade €110 / C$172 if you want door-to-terminal.

Before you book

  • Flights Choose a Toronto departure after 6:00 PM on 24 September, and a Milan Malpensa departure near 1:00 PM on 4 October. Air Canada, Lufthansa, and Air France/KLM are the usual patterns.
  • Tickets Colosseum, Uffizi, Duomo rooftop, Doge’s Palace, and Via dell’Amore sell out. Reserve as soon as official calendars open.
  • Vegetarian Rome and Florence are easy. Ask hotels to note preferences. Borgo San Jacopo already has a vegetarian tasting menu.
  • Luggage One medium suitcase each. Cinque Terre and Venice punish oversized bags. La Torretta’s porter is booked with the room.
  • Pace This itinerary is rich, not slow. If you later want to cut a city, drop Milan and fly home from Venice, or drop Cinque Terre and take a second night in Venice.
